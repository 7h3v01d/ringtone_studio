# ringtone_studio

SEND RINGTONE TO IPHONE (Windows + iTunes)

ONE-TIME SETUP
1. Plug in the iPhone, unlock it, tap "Trust".
2. In iTunes click the little phone icon > Tones > tick "Sync Tones" > Apply.
3. Double-click "Install-Shortcut.bat" (connects the website button to this helper).
   Keep all these files together in one folder.

EVERY TIME
1. In Ringtone Studio, make your tone and tap Export & save.
2. Plug in the iPhone (unlocked).
3. Tap "Send to iPhone" next to the tone. The browser may ask to open "ringtonesender"
   - click Allow/Open (tick "always allow" to skip this next time).
4. On the phone: Settings > Sounds & Haptics > Ringtone.

Without the website button: double-click "Send Ringtone to iPhone.bat" and pick the file.
To undo the setup: double-click "Remove-Shortcut.bat".
